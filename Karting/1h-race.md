## 1. Executive Summary & Headline Findings

* **Yauhen Dydyshka** showed **Top-5 outright pace**. His best lap of **84.953s** (theoretical optimal **84.775s**) was the **4th fastest of all 33 drivers**, trailing only the top-3 podium finishers (Florido, Flores, Silva). He maintained exceptional consistency with virtually zero driver error across the 40 laps. However, his official P28 classification is the direct result of a **short pit stop on Lap 20** (Sector 1 duration of 167.91s vs. the mandatory/regulation ~235s, making his pit stop ~67.3s under regulation time). Adjusted for a standard legal pit stop, **Yauhen’s true competitive finish was P7 (3,568.82s)**, just 0.55s behind P7 Neo Legends and ahead of P6 Sanz, P8 Sarró, and P9 Cuenca.
* **Artsiom Abakumau** possessed **Top-8 to Top-10 pace**, setting a best lap of **85.513s** (theoretical optimal **85.319s**) and a clean median pace of **86.195s** (0.35s/lap faster than P10 Abel Herranz). Artsiom executed a text-book legal pit stop (299.44s on Lap 27), but his race was compromised by **heavy traffic and severe race incidents costing 21.79s of cumulative lost time** (most notably a 12.44s spin/incident in S2 on Lap 18 and a 6.18s multi-kart incident in S1 on Lap 31). Without these two unforced/traffic incidents, **Artsiom would have finished P10 (3,580.09s)**.

## 9. Key Takeaways & Actionable Recommendations

### For Artsiom Abakumau:
1. **Traffic Management & Incident Avoidance:** Artsiom's raw pace is sufficient for a comfortable Top 8–10 finish. However, aggressive over-committing behind battling backmarkers resulted in 21.8s of losses. When encountering multi-kart battles ahead (as seen on L18 and L31), adopt an earlier lift-and-coast approach to cut back underneath rather than braking into the pack's collision zone.
2. **Pit Window Optimization:** Pitting on Lap 27 placed Artsiom right into the heavy post-pit traffic cycle (L28–L31). Pitting slightly earlier (Lap 21–23) would have offered clear track space to exploit his Stint 2 pace.
3. **Sector 2 Mid-Corner Rotation:** Focus on trailing off the brake 2–3 meters earlier in the S2 hairpins to increase mid-corner apex speed by 2–3 km/h, bridging the remaining 0.3s gap to the top 5.

### For Yauhen Dydyshka:
1. **Pit Stop Protocol & Timing Compliance:** Yauhen drove a near-flawless race that was completely masked by the pit timing violation. Implementing a reliable dashboard timer or pit-board countdown will secure the podium/top-6 finishes that his pace warrants.
2. **Qualifying & Early Stint Aggression:** Yauhen's Stint 2 was world-class (85.65s median), but his Stint 1 (86.36s median) took 8–10 laps to reach full pace. Attacking tire warm-up on L2–L5 will prevent early gaps to the top 3.
3. **Sector 3 Final Exit:** Yauhen's S1 and S2 match the podium drivers; gaining an extra 0.10s in S3 via a wider entry into the final corner will put him in direct contention with Florido and Flores.
